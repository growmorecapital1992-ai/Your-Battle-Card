### 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Allows competitive gaming athletes, guild leaders, and casual players to forge an intimidating, tournament-grade esports card. Users upload portrait photos, toggle 7 modular face upgrades, choose an Awakened Shadow Titan evolving behind them from 9 legendary archetypes (or brainstorm via AI), forge an Ultimate Gamer Name, pick battle-tested roles, select 3 paramount combat stats, and customize terrifying quotes.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: Competitive battle royale and tactical shooter players (Free Fire, BGMI/PUBG, Valorant, Apex Legends, COD Mobile), guild captains, tournament organizers, and esports creators wanting showcase profile assets.
- **Key Value**: Replaces generic profile screenshots with a ferocious, cinematic collectible card that commands respect and fear in lobbies.

### 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: Modular 7-Upgrade Face System**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Implement SVG/Canvas overlays and CSS composite filters for the 7 face upgrades (e.g. vector dark sunglasses, glowing red pupil streaks, procedural scar cuts, vignette shadow layers, neon rim glow filter).
  - *Why*: Provides instant 60fps responsiveness as the user toggles and tweaks each upgrade without lag or waiting for network round-trips.
- **Decision 2: 9 Pre-Crafted Ultimate Entities + AI Custom Generator**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Deliver 9 handcrafted, visually distinct SVG/Canvas composite shadow entities (complete with glowing eyes, capes, chains, mist) plus a server-side Gemini 3.8 Flash API route to generate custom ultimate battle titles, intimidating awakening titles, and combat lore.
  - *Why*: Guarantees instant stunning visual results out of the box while allowing limitless AI creative extension.
- **Decision 3: Ultimate Gamer Alias Forge with Esports Presets**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Provide both instant 1-click presets (e.g. clicking `King [XYZ]` or `Mr. [XYZ] "BOSS"` immediately formats the name) and individual dropdowns/buttons for prefix, title, suffix, and esports symbols (亗, ꧁, ☬, 👑).
  - *Why*: Matches the exact Free Fire/BGMI aesthetic shown in the user's reference screenshot.
- **Decision 4: High-Resolution Client-Side Export via Canvas/html2canvas**:
  - *Chosen Approach*: Capture the DOM card element at 2x scale, ensuring crisp text, glowing eye effects, and full filter retention in downloaded PNGs.
  - *Why*: Pure client-side export executes offline, works instantaneously, and guarantees that exact user filter parameters match the saved file.
